## Workspace Entry: 2026-10-17
## Weekly Product Pulse

### Summary of Top Themes
1. **DP Charges & Fees**: High user dissatisfaction surrounding unexpected fee deductions during stock sell execution.
2. **App Performance/Lag**: Intermittent latency during peak market opening hours.
3. **Customer Support**: Delayed turnarounds for query resolutions.

### Supporting User Quotes
> "Why was ₹15.93 deducted from my account when I sold my shares? Groww says zero brokerage but they are charging hidden fees every time I sell!"
> "Groww claims no hidden charges, but I noticed a DP charge being deducted on my sell orders."
> "I hate that every time I sell even a single stock, ₹15.93 is gone as a Depository Participant charge."

### Key Observation
Users perceive DP (Depository Participant) charges as "hidden fees" due to zero-brokerage marketing messaging. The fee deduction is not surfaced clearly before the user confirms a sell order.

### Action Ideas for Product Team
1. **Pre-trade Tooltip**: Display a breakdown itemizing DP charges directly on the sell confirmation screen.
2. **Zero-Brokerage Clarification Banner**: Add an inline banner on the pricing page explicitly clarifying exchange, regulatory, and DP fees versus Groww brokerage.
3. **Automated Support Bot Snippet**: Deploy a proactive chatbot banner when users submit tickets regarding sell balance deductions.


### Approved Support Explainer:
### Understanding Depository Participant (DP) Charges on Groww

- **What is a DP Charge?**: A flat fee charged by the depository (CDSL) and the depository participant (Groww) whenever you sell shares from your demat account.
- **When is it applied?**: DP charges are applicable **once per stock per trading day** upon selling, regardless of the quantity sold.
- **Groww Brokerage vs. DP Charge**: While Groww offers zero brokerage on delivery trades, DP charges are mandatory depository pass-through charges.
- **Current Fee Structure**: Standard DP charges are ₹13.5 + 18% GST (totaling ₹15.93) per company per day sold.
- **Transparency**: DP charges are deducted from your trade delivery realization balance at the end of the trading day.

**Last checked:** 2026-10-17
